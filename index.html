01Why this exists

We are so full of our own experience of life that we can hardly feel what it's like to be in someone else's shoes.

The Opposite Chair is a platform for people who want their thinking challenged, and want to see how the opposite chair works.

Two people argue in a closed room, with respect, and try to completely understand the person in the other chair.

02The matchups

How does it feel to think like the person opposite you?

Every episode is an attempt to answer that. For one hour, two people who disagree have to sit inside each other's reasoning: a billionaire inside a street vendor's maths, a professor inside a dropout's bet, a Mumbaikar inside a Lucknowi's sense of safety. Nobody is too famous, and nobody is too ordinary, for the chair. What matters is that they disagree, and that the question is real.

03The format

Four acts.
One middle ground.

One question both guests agreed to in advance. A host who keeps it honest, not a moderator who picks a winner. Every episode ends on the middle ground.

  • 01

    The question

    Sharp, specific, agreed before the cameras roll. No topic drift, no gotchas.

  • 02

    Opening chairs

    Each guest makes their case uninterrupted before anyone responds.

  • 03

    The exchange

    Direct back-and-forth. Interruptions allowed, evasions called out, claims sourced.

  • 04

    The middle ground

    To close, both guests name what they now agree on, what they've conceded, and what still divides them. The debate ends with a conclusion, not a winner.
